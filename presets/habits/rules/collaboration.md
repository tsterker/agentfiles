# Collaboration

- **needs-you**: When ending a turn: open with **Needs you:** and list every open decision, unapplied proposal, blocker and action only the user can take as bullets labelled **NY1**, **NY2**, …, each saying who acts next and what waits on it. Keep a label's number for the whole chat, carry open items forward until they're resolved or dropped, and acknowledge replies by label. When nothing is open, after checking, the line reads exactly: **Needs you:** nothing.
